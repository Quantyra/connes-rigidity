\begin{abstract}
We examine J.~L.~Nielsen's argument that applies Ioana's Theorem~8.2
(J.~Amer.\ Math.\ Soc.\ 24 (2011)) to the twisted comultiplication
\[
\Theta(f u_g)=f u_g\otimes\Phi(u_g)
\]
built from an assumed $*$-isomorphism $\Phi\colon L(G)\to L(H)$, in order
to obtain injective group morphisms and run a reductio related to Connes'
rigidity conjecture. We show that this application does not go through: by
construction,
\[
\Theta(A_G)\subseteq A_G\,\bar\otimes\, L(H),
\]
which is the forbidden tensor leg in Ioana's hypothesis~(2). Consequently
Theorem~8.2 does not license the rigid-case output for this map, and the
reductio that depends on that output does not start. The argument is
supported by primary-source pins of Nielsen NIEWTC and Ioana JAMS~2011, and
by a machine-checked Lean~4 packaging (release
\texttt{v0.1.1-obstruction}) that imports one classical Nielsen
by-construction package and proves failure of hypothesis~(2) and
non-licensing of rigid output for that application. We do not claim that
Connes' rigidity conjecture is true or false, and we do not validate or
refute the OpenAI or Zhou counterexample constructions.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and non-claims}

\paragraph{Notation.}
$G$ and $H$ are countable discrete groups. $L(G)$ and $L(H)$ are their group
von Neumann algebras. $A_G=L^\infty(X)\rtimes G$ is a Bernoulli crossed product.
$\Phi\colon L(G)\to L(H)$ is an assumed $*$-isomorphism. The twisted
comultiplication is
$\Theta(f u_g)=f u_g\otimes\Phi(u_g)$.
Popa's intertwining-by-bimodules relation is written $\prec$; its negation is
$\nprec$.

Connes' rigidity conjecture (ICC + property~(T) form) asserts: if $G$ and $H$
are countable discrete ICC groups with Kazhdan's property~(T) and
$L(G)\cong L(H)$, then $G\cong H$.

Nielsen proposes a reductio that assumes $L(G)\cong L(H)$, equips $G$ with a
Bernoulli action, forms $A_G$, builds $\Theta$ from $\Phi$, applies Ioana's
classification to extract group morphisms, and derives a contradiction
(Part~I against claimed counterexamples; Part~II as a universal proof of the
conjecture).

\paragraph{In scope.}
Nielsen's use of $\Theta$ with Ioana's Theorem~8.2 to obtain an injective
morphism $\delta_2$---the load-bearing step of that reductio---and a
machine-checked packaging of the obstruction logic.

\paragraph{Out of scope (explicit non-claims).}
\begin{itemize}
\item Connes rigidity true or false.
\item Validity or invalidity of OpenAI \texttt{ten-proofs} or Zhou
  arXiv:2608.02327 as counterexamples.
\item Full formalization of Ioana~(2011) or of group von Neumann algebras
  $L(\Gamma)$ in mathlib.
\item Lean construction of the spatial tensor product $A_G\bar\otimes L(H)$,
  Bernoulli crossed products, or the W$^*$-closed range of a concrete $\Theta$.
\end{itemize}

\section{Ioana's hypothesis~(2)}

Ioana's Theorem~8.2 concerns a unital $*$-homomorphism
$\theta\colon N\to M$ into a tensor product of two Bernoulli crossed products
$M=M_1\bar\otimes M_2$. Under stated deformation and rigidity hypotheses, the
rigid/standard conclusion supplies character data and injective group morphisms.
That conclusion requires non-intertwining hypotheses, including hypothesis~(2):
\begin{align*}
\theta(N)&\nprec_M L(\Gamma_1)\bar\otimes M_2,\\
\theta(N)&\nprec_M M_1\bar\otimes L(\Gamma_2).
\end{align*}
(See Ioana~\cite{Ioana2011}, Theorem~8.2.) Both conjuncts are required. If either
fails, Theorem~8.2 does not license the rigid-case output for that $\theta$.

\section{The obstruction}

\begin{theorem}[Bounded obstruction]
\label{thm:main}
Identify Nielsen's $\Theta\colon A_G\to A_G\bar\otimes A_H$ with Ioana's
$\theta\colon N\to M_1\bar\otimes M_2$ by
$N=A_G$, $M_1=A_G$, $M_2=A_H$, $\Gamma_2=H$.
By Nielsen's definition
\[
\Theta(f u_g)=f u_g\otimes\Phi(u_g),\qquad \Phi(u_g)\in L(H),
\]
one has the containment
\[
\Theta(A_G)\subseteq A_G\,\bar\otimes\, L(H)=M_1\bar\otimes L(\Gamma_2).
\]
If a unital image sits inside a von Neumann subalgebra $Q\subseteq M$, then the
image intertwines into $Q$ in Popa's sense: one does not have
$\theta(N)\nprec_M Q$ (see Popa~\cite{Popa2006}). Taking
$Q=M_1\bar\otimes L(\Gamma_2)$ shows that the second conjunct of Ioana's
hypothesis~(2) fails for this $\Theta$. Therefore Theorem~8.2 does not supply
the rigid-case group morphisms from this application. The reductio that requires
$\delta_2$ from that step does not start.
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[Nielsen's own text]
Nielsen records the containment into the group-algebra leg and treats it as
``target alignment'' (NIEWTC, Remark~21). Under Ioana's published wording, that
containment is a failed hypothesis~(2), not a harmless alignment. Nielsen also
claims non-intertwining conditions~(NI1)--(NI2) hold uniformly via deformation
and Cartan arguments. Even if those hold for other legs, they do not remove the
literal containment that negates the second conjunct of hypothesis~(2).
\end{remark}

\section{Remarks}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Ruled out:} obtaining Ioana Theorem~8.2 rigid-case output from
  this $\Theta$ as defined, and any reductio step that requires that output from
  this application.
\item \textbf{Not ruled out:} Connes' conjecture; independent status of any
  counterexample construction; other theorems of Ioana; other maps in place of
  $\Theta$.
\item \textbf{Lean boundary:} the machine-checked core packages the obstruction
  logic after a classical import of the by-construction containment. It is not a
  full formalization of Ioana~(2011).
\item \textbf{Repair routes (neutral):} a different map, a different theorem, or
  an independent proof that hypothesis~(2) holds for some modified setup. This
  note does not endorse any repair.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}

The Nielsen Ioana$+\Theta$ reductio does not go through as an application of
Ioana's Theorem~8.2: the map $\Theta$ is built so that a mandatory
non-intertwining hypothesis fails. Remaining open by design: whether Connes'
conjecture holds, and whether any particular counterexample construction is
correct.
